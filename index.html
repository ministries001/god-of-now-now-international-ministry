About Our Ministry

A Family Built on Faith, Love & Purpose

God of Now Now International Ministry is committed to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ and raising believers who are spiritually strong, purpose-driven and passionate about God's kingdom.

Through worship, prayer, teaching, evangelism, discipleship and community outreach, we seek to demonstrate the love of Christ and impact lives.
