Leadership With Purpose

At God of Now Now International Ministry, we believe that leadership is a divine responsibility and a call to serve.

Our leadership is committed to spiritual growth, sound biblical teaching, prayer, evangelism, discipleship and the transformation of lives through the power of God.

A Calling To Serve

Leadership Is Service

We believe that true spiritual leadership is not about position but about service, sacrifice and responsibility.

Our leaders seek to lead by example, encourage believers, strengthen families, develop ministries and reach people with the message of Jesus Christ.

Every leader is encouraged to remain committed to prayer, God's Word, integrity, humility and the advancement of God's Kingdom.
