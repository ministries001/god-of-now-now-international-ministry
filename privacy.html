Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to God of Now Now International Ministry. This page explains how information submitted through our website may be handled.

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

1. Introduction

God of Now Now International Ministry respects the privacy of everyone who visits or interacts with this website.

This Privacy Policy explains the general ways in which information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Your name.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address, where provided.
  • Prayer requests.
  • Testimonies submitted through the website.
  • Messages or enquiries sent through contact forms.
  • Other information you voluntarily provide.

3. Prayer Requests

Prayer requests may contain personal or sensitive information. We encourage visitors to share only information that they are comfortable providing.

Prayer requests are intended to be used for ministry, prayer and pastoral purposes.

4. Testimonies

If you submit a testimony, the ministry may review the information before deciding whether it can be published on the church website, social media platforms or other ministry communication channels.

Personal information will not intentionally be published as part of a testimony without appropriate consideration of the information provided and any permission required.

5. How We May Use Information

Information voluntarily provided to the ministry may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Respond to prayer requests.
  • Communicate with members and visitors.
  • Process ministry-related requests.
  • Improve the church website.
  • Organise church activities and programmes.
  • Provide information about church events and activities.

6. Information Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect information provided to the ministry from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

Some parts of the website may use third-party services such as video platforms, social media platforms, mapping services, hosting providers or other external services.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage visitors to review the policies of external services when using them.

8. Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, performance or analytics.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

9. Children's Privacy

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of online services.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children through this website.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media pages and other online resources.

God of Now Now International Ministry is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

11. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information when using the website. However, some services or features may require information in order to respond to your request.

If you have previously provided information to the ministry and would like to ask about it, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

The ministry may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the website, ministry operations or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information submitted through the website is handled, please contact the church.
