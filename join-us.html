You Are Welcome Here

At God of Now Now International Ministry, we believe that everyone has a place in God's house. Whether you are visiting for the first time, returning to church, or looking for a spiritual family, we are delighted to welcome you.

Our desire is to help people encounter God, grow in His Word, discover their purpose and become everything God has called them to be.
