Ministries That Make A Difference

Prayer Ministry

Men's Ministry

Helping men become strong men of faith, responsible leaders and positive influences in their homes and communities.

👩

Women's Ministry

Empowering women spiritually and practically while encouraging fellowship, purpose, leadership and service.

Youth Ministry
