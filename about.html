About Our Ministry

Welcome to God of Now Now International Ministry, a family of believers committed to knowing Christ, growing in faith and making a difference in our world.

A Place of Encounter
Who We Are

We Believe God Is Still Doing Great Things Today

God of Now Now International Ministry is a Christian ministry established with a passion to see people encounter God, experience His love and walk in the purpose He has given them.

We are a growing family of believers who believe in the power of prayer, the authority of God's Word and the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

Our church is a place where people from different backgrounds can come together to worship God, learn His Word, build meaningful relationships and serve their communities.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Everything we do is centered on Christ and His purpose for humanity.

👁

Our Vision

To raise a generation of Christ-centered believers who know God, understand their identity in Christ, discover their purpose and positively impact their families, communities and the world.

🎯

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, disciple believers, build strong families, empower people through God's Word and demonstrate the love of Christ through practical service and outreach.

Our Faith

What We Believe

Our faith is founded on the Gospel of Jesus Christ and the truth of God's Word.

We desire to help every believer develop a genuine relationship with God and live out their faith every day.

  • We believe in one true and living God.
  • We believe in Jesus Christ, the Son of God and Savior of the world.
  • We believe in the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  • We believe in the Holy Spirit and His work in the life of every believer.
  • We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God.
  • We believe in salvation through faith in Jesus Christ.
  • We believe believers are called to live holy and purposeful lives.
  • We believe in the power of prayer and faith.
Making a Difference

Faith That Serves People

Our faith is not only something we express inside the church. We believe that the love of Christ should be demonstrated through practical acts of service.

Through evangelism, prayer, community outreach, youth development and other initiatives, we seek opportunities to make a positive difference in the lives of people around us.

We believe that when God's people serve with love, communities can be transformed.
