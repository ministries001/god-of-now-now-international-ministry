Your Giving Makes an Impact

Giving is an important part of our worship and our commitment to the work of God. Through your generosity, we are able to support ministry activities, outreach programmes, community projects and the preaching of the Gospel.

Every gift matters. Whether large or small, your giving contributes to the advancement of God's work and helps us reach more lives with hope, faith and love.

Giving Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers about giving to the ministry.

Can I give through bank transfer?

Yes. You can make your tithe, offering or special gift through any of the official church bank accounts listed on this page.

What can my giving support?

Your giving can support the ministry's Gospel work, outreach activities, missions, community support, church programmes and other ministry needs.

Does every amount make a difference?

Yes. Every sincere gift helps contribute to the work of the ministry. Give according to your ability and with a willing heart.

How can I contact the church about my giving?

You can use the Contact page to reach the ministry directly for questions or further information.
