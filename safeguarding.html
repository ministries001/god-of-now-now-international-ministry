Safeguarding

Protecting children, young people, vulnerable adults and everyone who worships, serves and participates in our ministry.

A Safe Place to Worship

God of Now Now International Ministry is committed to creating a respectful, welcoming and safe environment where people can worship God, participate in ministry and grow in faith without fear of abuse, exploitation, harassment or inappropriate conduct.

🛡️

Protection

We encourage practices that help protect children, young people, vulnerable adults, members, visitors and ministry workers.

🤝

Respect

Every person should be treated with dignity, kindness, fairness and respect regardless of age or background.

❤️

Care

We seek to respond responsibly to concerns and provide appropriate pastoral support while respecting confidentiality and safety.

1. Our Commitment

The safety and wellbeing of people who participate in the ministry are important to us. Church leaders, workers and volunteers are expected to conduct themselves responsibly and appropriately.

We encourage a culture where concerns can be raised without intimidation or retaliation.

2. Children and Young People

Children and young people deserve particular care and protection. Adults serving in children or youth activities should maintain appropriate boundaries and follow responsible ministry practices.

  • Children should be treated with dignity and respect.
  • Adults should maintain appropriate physical and emotional boundaries.
  • Concerns about a child's safety should be taken seriously.
  • Parents and guardians should be kept appropriately informed about ministry activities.

3. Vulnerable Adults

People who may require additional support or protection should be treated with dignity, patience and respect.

Ministry workers should avoid exploiting a person's vulnerability, dependence or trust.

4. Responsible Conduct

All ministry workers, leaders and volunteers are expected to act in a manner that protects the dignity and wellbeing of others.

  • Do not abuse a position of trust or authority.
  • Do not engage in threatening, humiliating or abusive behaviour.
  • Do not exploit people financially, emotionally or spiritually.
  • Maintain appropriate boundaries in pastoral relationships.
  • Respect personal information and confidentiality.
  • Report serious safeguarding concerns through appropriate channels.

5. Abuse and Exploitation

Safeguarding concerns may include physical abuse, sexual abuse, emotional or psychological abuse, neglect, exploitation, harassment, intimidation, financial exploitation or other forms of harmful behaviour.

If Someone Is in Immediate Danger

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency services or relevant authorities first. Do not put yourself or another person at additional risk.

6. Raising a Concern

Anyone who has a safeguarding concern involving a church activity, worker, volunteer, leader, member or visitor is encouraged to raise the concern promptly.

Where possible, provide clear information about what happened, when it happened and who may be affected. Do not investigate serious allegations yourself.

7. Confidentiality

Safeguarding information should be handled carefully and shared only with people who need the information to respond appropriately.

Confidentiality cannot always be guaranteed where disclosure is necessary to protect a person from serious harm or where reporting to an appropriate authority is required.

8. Online Safety

The same standards of respect and appropriate conduct apply to online ministry activities, social media communication, messaging, video calls and other digital interactions.

  • Do not send inappropriate content to children or vulnerable people.
  • Do not request unnecessary private information.
  • Maintain appropriate communication boundaries.
  • Be careful when sharing photographs or videos of others.

9. Photographs and Media

Church services and events may sometimes be photographed or recorded for ministry communication and documentation.

Reasonable care should be taken when photographing or publishing images involving children and vulnerable people.

10. Volunteers and Ministry Workers

People serving in ministry should understand their responsibilities and conduct themselves in accordance with church leadership, applicable policies and relevant laws.

Where appropriate, the ministry may introduce additional procedures for roles involving children, young people or vulnerable adults.

11. False or Malicious Reports

Safeguarding concerns should always be taken seriously. However, knowingly making a false allegation or deliberately providing misleading information can cause serious harm.

Concerns should therefore be raised honestly and responsibly.

12. Cooperation With Authorities

Where a safeguarding matter involves conduct that may constitute a crime or serious risk of harm, the ministry may encourage or cooperate with appropriate authorities and professional services, in accordance with applicable law.

Need to Report a Concern?

If you have a safeguarding concern relating to the church, please contact the ministry using the official contact details. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service or authority.

Please do not use social media comments to report sensitive safeguarding concerns.
